# AGOL-Enterprise Admin Kit — Support

A Windows desktop application for auditing and maintaining ArcGIS Online and
ArcGIS Enterprise organizations: taking inventory of content, reporting
findings with the evidence behind them, backing items up, and transferring a
departing member's content to someone else with a record that can be undone.

## Getting help

Email **gaqo@outlook.com**. It helps to include:

- what you were trying to do,
- what the application said, word for word,
- whether the portal is ArcGIS Online or ArcGIS Enterprise, and which version.

Please do not attach passwords, tokens, or files from the application's storage
folder. None of those are needed to diagnose a problem, and the storage folder
contains your sign-in tokens.

The application ships with help in fourteen languages, reachable from the Help
page inside it. That is the fastest answer for most questions.

## Privacy

The application collects nothing — no analytics, no telemetry, no crash
reporting. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy) for what it stores on your own
computer and what it sends to your portal.

## Reporting something that looks wrong

If the application reports a finding you believe is mistaken, the finding
carries the evidence it was based on — an item id, a service URL, a duplicate
pair. Including that evidence in your email makes the difference between a
guess and a fix.

# AGOL-Enterprise Admin Kit — 支持

一款用于审计和维护 ArcGIS Online 与 ArcGIS Enterprise 组织的 Windows 桌面应用：清点内容、给出带证据的发现项、备份条目，以及把离职成员的内容转移给他人并留下可撤销的记录。

## 获取帮助

发邮件至 **gaqo@outlook.com**。附上这些信息会更快：

- 你当时想做什么；
- 应用原话说了什么（请照抄）；
- 门户是 ArcGIS Online 还是 ArcGIS Enterprise，以及版本号。

请**不要**附上密码、令牌，或应用存储文件夹里的文件。诊断问题不需要这些，而那个文件夹里存着你的登录令牌。

应用内置了十四种语言的帮助文档，从应用里的"帮助"页面即可打开。大多数问题在那里能最快找到答案。

## 隐私

本应用不收集任何数据——没有分析统计、没有遥测、没有崩溃上报。应用在你自己电脑上保存了什么、向你的门户发送了什么，详见[隐私政策](privacy)。

## 报告你认为不对的结果

如果应用给出的某个发现项你认为是误判，该发现项本身带着它所依据的证据——条目 ID、服务地址、重复配对。把这些证据一并写进邮件，是"猜测"和"修好"之间的区别。
